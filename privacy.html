FCbot Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

This policy explains how FCbot handles user information.

1. Information Collection

FCbot may receive information made available through Discord when users interact with the bot. The actual information collected depends on the bot's features and configuration.

2. Use of Information

Information is used only for the bot's intended functionality and operation.

3. Data Storage and Sharing

The developer must document any information stored, its retention period, and any third parties it is shared with. Contact the developer for details.

4. Data Security

Reasonable measures should be taken to protect any information handled by FCbot.

5. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the FCbot developer through the official support channel.
